# Chief of Staff Brief — 2026-08-28: Narrow-Audience PMF Plan

Synthesis of Product, Analytics, Marketing, and Sales's independent PMF research
(`ops/product/roadmap/pmf-narrow-audience.md`, `ops/analytics/digests/pmf-measurement-plan.md`,
`ops/marketing/strategy/pmf-narrow-cohort-recruitment.md`, `ops/sales/pipeline/pmf-institutional-pilot.md`).

## Headline finding: three departments converged on the same audience independently

Product, Marketing, and Sales each proposed a narrow niche without seeing each other's work.
All three landed in the same place: **final-year CS/IT students at non-elite (non-IIT/NIT)
tier-2/3 engineering colleges, in an active campus placement cycle, who've never sat a real
interview.** That's a real confidence signal, not a coincidence — worth treating this as the
default niche unless you have specific network/data reasons to override it.

## Recommended audience

Same as above. Rationale that held across departments: this is who the product's ICP-first
redesign was already built for (landing page tags, lead testimonial); campus placement season
(Aug-Dec, i.e. now) is a hard calendar deadline that drives the "repeat usage before a real
interview" signal; tier-2/3 (not IIT/NIT) colleges carry the actual knowledge/expectation gap
the product targets — elite colleges already have placement-cell drilling.

Two follow-up tests once this lands real data: Product's Slice B (same cohort, HR/behavioral
round instead of technical — zero new build, tests whether the product's differentiation holds
outside DSA prep) and Slice C (off-campus early-career switchers — the dedicated willingness-to-
pay probe, since campus students have no disposable income and will read as a WTP false negative).

## Phased plan

**Phase 0 — before recruiting anyone (Tech pod, routes through a new dev session, not this one).**
Two small instrumentation gaps block a measurable read entirely, per both Product and Analytics
independently: (1) nothing captures *why* a user is here or *when their real interview is* — so
"they came back" can't be told apart from "they came back because it mattered"; (2) no acquisition-
source/referral capture at all. Both are additive, no pricing/legal/external-messaging touch. Also:
a live smoke-test pass on the debrief pipeline with a real `GROQ_API_KEY` (several paths — drill
loop, cross-session trends, the TPM fix — are still unverified live per `CLAUDE.md`'s own status
table), and closing the mobile-verification gap, since this audience skews mobile-heavy. Running a
real pilot cohort into an unverified pipeline reads as "this audience doesn't want it" when the
real cause is a bug — Product, Analytics, and Sales all flagged this same risk independently.

**Phase 1 (weeks 1-3) — recruitment + measurement, once Phase 0 gaps close.**
Marketing's plan: club coordinators at 2 target colleges (primary, highest trust-per-reach) +
Anshuman's warm network (parallel, cheap, caps at ~20-40 people) + one placement-prep community
and one Reddit post (secondary). 3-week funnel estimate: ~400-700 reached → ~35-60 signups →
~20-35 completed sessions (downside case as low as 15-25 if community/coordinator permission is
slow). Analytics stands up the Sean Ellis survey in parallel (external form, no engineering needed) —
exact wording: *"How would you feel if you could no longer use PrepSignals?"*, delivered 3-5 days
after a user's first completed debrief.

**Phase 1, parallel track (contingent, your call) — institutional pilot.**
Sales' read: a single institutional partnership (one bootcamp career team preferred over a college
TPO — smaller, more homogeneous cohort, sharper incentive) could reach 30-100 in-ICP users at zero
CAC inside 3-4 weeks — faster density than organic recruitment alone. The real risk Sales flagged:
mistaking the institution's yes for the product's PMF signal. Structure required if you approve this:
opt-in usage only (track assigned-vs-opted-in separately if the institution promotes to everyone),
individual-level engagement is the actual win condition, institutional satisfaction is a secondary
signal only. Needs Director of Compliance sign-off on the aggregate-reporting data-sharing design
before any institution is contacted (DPDP-relevant even in aggregate form) — and this sits ahead of
Sales' formal Phase 3 (gated on Monetisation shipping), so running it now is an explicit sequencing
exception, not something Sales should just proceed on.

**Phase 2 (end of week 3) — read the data, decide.**
Check against the bundle below. Decide: double down on this niche (more colleges), run the Slice B
fast-follow (behavioral round, same cohort), or pivot the niche entirely.

## Metrics to track

| Signal | Definition | Target | Status today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activation | Completed one full mock → rendered debrief | — (denominator for everything else) | Computable today (`debriefed_sessions / total_sessions`) |
| Core retention | Returned **because a real interview was approaching** — not just returned | ≥50% of activated users, 2+ sessions before their interview date | **Not measurable yet** — needs the interview-date/intent capture from Phase 0 |
| Depth of use | Used drill/retry loop after a debrief with a signal ≤3 | Non-trivial % of eligible users | Event exists (`drill_used`); denominator needs a manual DB join for now |
| Referral/advocacy | Unprompted friend referral | ≥1 in 5 pilot users | No infra — ask directly in survey, or just watch for it manually at this scale |
| Sean Ellis score | "How would you feel if you could no longer use PrepSignals?" (asked as: if you couldn't use it before your next real interview) | ≥40% "very disappointed" — **but only trust this on 40+ responses**, meaning target ~150-200 activated users, not 40-50 | No survey infra exists — external form (Typeform/Google Form), manual send |
| Willingness to pay | Explicit ask or fake-door click, pre-payment-rail | ≥15-20% of engaged users — **read this on Slice C (off-campus), not the campus cohort** | No probe exists anywhere in the product yet |
| Outcome | `actual_outcome` — did the debrief's verdict track reality | Directional only, slow, low response rate | API exists, zero UI, zero Mixpanel visibility — manual email ask is proportionate at this scale |

## What defines a successful PMF read

**Not any single metric alone** — all three reports converged on this. Genuine signal = the
bundle together: Sean Ellis ≥40% on a real sample (not 12 responses that happen to clear 40%),
meaningful `session_number ≥2` tied to an actual upcoming interview (not idle re-engagement),
non-trivial drill-loop usage among users who got a weak signal, and — weighted more heavily than
usual at this scale — unprompted qualitative pull: people asking to bring a friend, asking when
paid tiers ship, referencing specific debrief feedback back to you unprompted.

**False positives to guard against**, named specifically by Analytics: high signup with a weak
completed-debrief rate (vanity funnel); returning users with no real interview driving the return;
a friendly Sean Ellis score from a cohort that's mostly personal network feeling obligated to be
positive; drill-loop rating improvements with no independent evidence of real skill change; strong
quantitative numbers with zero qualitative pull — genuine PMF at this scale is usually loud, not
just statistically present.

## Addendum (same day): formulas, funnel decomposition, adaptation plan

Follow-up from Anshuman after reviewing the plan above: wanted the metrics as precise formulas
(not prose targets), the funnel explicitly decomposed and connected to those metrics, and an
answer to whether this plan can actually adapt if real behavior diverges from the model. Full
version with tables lives in the artifact; the two findings worth having in the written record:

**Decomposing the funnel surfaces a real numeric shortfall the prose version hid.** Chaining
Marketing's stage-by-stage funnel estimate forward: Reached (400-700) → Clicked (~15-20% CTR) →
Signed up (~45-55% of clicks) → Activated/completed (~55-65% of signups) lands at **20-35
activated users by week 3**. Analytics set the Sean Ellis sample floor at **150-200 activated
users** (40+ responses needed at a realistic 20-30% response rate). The funnel as currently scoped
reaches roughly 15-20% of that floor in the 3-week window. This doesn't invalidate the plan — it
means the week-3 checkpoint should be read as directional/qualitative, not a statistically firm
Sean Ellis verdict, unless the funnel gets wider (more colleges/channels) or the window extends.
Added as decision item 7 above.

**Formulas** (numerator/denominator for each metric — full table with targets is in the artifact):
Activation = completed debriefs ÷ sessions started. Core Retention = users with a 2nd+ session
before their stated interview date ÷ activated users. Depth of Use = drill-loop users ÷ users with
a drill-eligible signal (≤3). Referral = unincentivized referrers ÷ activated users. Sean Ellis =
"very disappointed" responses ÷ total responses, valid only at n≥40. Willingness to Pay = yes/click
responses ÷ surveyed, computed on the off-campus slice only. Outcome Calibration = agreement
between verdict bucket and actual_outcome ÷ reported outcomes — directional only, no numeric target.

**Adaptation plan — the plan was linear, now it branches.** Seven trigger → likely-meaning →
response pairs added (full table in the artifact), covering: message/audience mismatch (fix
messaging, not product), pipeline breakage disguised as an audience signal (check Phase 0's
smoke-test first), one-and-done usage weakening the retention thesis (be ready to revise the
metric, not just the channel), an undershooting sample (extend rather than report false precision),
organic pull skewing toward a different slice (follow it — that's the intended outcome of
narrow-first testing, not a failure), strong engagement with near-zero WTP (revisit institutional
pilot as a business model, not just distribution), and institutional satisfaction without individual
opt-in (discount that signal entirely — the trap Sales already flagged).
